How telemetry works

  1. Register a device here and copy its ID and token.
  2. Enter those in the mining app's Management settings. The app sends statistics only — never wallet keys.
  3. The device POSTs to /api/devices/{id}/telemetry every 10 seconds with Authorization: Bearer {token}.
  4. This dashboard polls the API every 5 seconds and displays only what devices actually report.

Estimated rewards are never presented as balances. Confirm pool balances and wallet payouts on the pool and in your own wallet.
